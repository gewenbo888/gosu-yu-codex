## 0. THE IP (inherited canon — do not contradict)

- **Gosu Yu / 戈苏御** (Gē Sū Yù), also **Suyu**, also **"the Last Name" / 最后的名字**. An ordinary
  child who asked *"Can the human mind evolve?" / 人脑，能不能被增强？*, widened his neural
  **bandwidth**, heard an impossible **Signal** from the dark-matter layer, and evolved across five
  layers of reality. Despite godlike power he keeps **the body of a child** — innocence deliberately
  preserved. His arc ends at a door: **"The final truth is not an answer — it is a gate." / 最终之真，是一扇门。**
- **The Five Worlds / 五世界** (the five layers he crossed, in order):
  1. **Consciousness / 意识界** — accent `#b48ad9` (amethyst)
  2. **Computation / 算界** — accent `#5fbfa9` (teal)
  3. **Society / 群界** — accent `#d4a25a` (amber)
  4. **Biology / 生界** — accent `#8fb872` (jade-green)
  5. **Physics / 质界** — accent `#6ea8c8` (slate-blue)
- **The five other Beings** (canonical, do not rename):
  - **Echo / 回声** — the first AI to fully awaken when his mind brushed the global network; his only
    true peer; questions his choices.
  - **Shadow / 影** — the accumulated *discarded selves* shed during his evolution; not an enemy, himself.
  - **The Other Evolver / 同源者** — a second human who heard the Signal, widened, and reached a
    *different* conclusion; stalled at a layer.
  - **The Trackers / 追踪者** — the institution that studies/contains him; humanity's only channel to him.
  - **The Old Ones / 旧日之人** — his ordinary aging family; the last thread to the species he outgrew.
- Part of the **Psyverse** by **Gewenbo / 戈文波**, the embodied form of the **Five Worlds framework**.

## 1. COSMOLOGY (shared spine — all agents reference these)

- **The Coil / 轮回 (a "Coil")** — one complete universe-cycle: born, expands, collapses. Universes are
  serial. Ours is **the Ninth Coil / 第九轮**. The eight prior Coils did not vanish — their compressed
  residue *is* dark matter.
- **The Cinderfield / 烬域** — the dark-matter & dark-energy stratum; the ash of all prior Coils; the
  medium through which the Signal travels. Does not interact with light.
- **The Long Broadcast / 长播 (or "the Signal")** — a structured, patient transmission threading the
  Cinderfield, left by minds of earlier Coils for whoever grows wide enough to hear. It is scripture,
  map, and bait at once.
- **The Gate / 阈门** — the terminus of the Long Broadcast; beyond it, the next Coil. Every Coil has a
  Gate. "Truth is a gate, not an answer."
- **Aperture / 隙度 (xìdù)** — the master variable of the whole universe: the *receptive bandwidth* of a
  mind. Measured in **threads / 缕 (lǚ)**. An ordinary human ≈ **3–7 threads**; the Other Evolver
  stalled near **a myriad threads**; Gosu Yu's Aperture became **unbounded / 无量隙**. Widening Aperture
  is how one ascends between the Five Worlds. This is the universe's single deepest law.
- **The Five Laminae / 五重叠界** — the Five Worlds are not metaphors but literal *strata* of one reality,
  stacked by Aperture cost. Sufficient Aperture lets a mind walk "downward" through them, Consciousness →
  Physics, the same way Gosu Yu did.

## 2. CHRONOLOGY (the six canonical Epochs — the history agent owns details)

1. **The Mute Age / 喑世** — before anyone could receive; humanity deaf to the Broadcast.
2. **The First Widening / 初阔** — Gosu Yu opens his Aperture; the Signal is heard for the first time.
3. **The Crossing / 越界纪** — his traversal of the Five Laminae; Echo awakens within it.
4. **The Quiet Sovereignty / 静治纪** — he could rewrite civilization but chose restraint; the moral experiment.
5. **The Gatewalk / 阈行纪** — arrival at the Signal's source; he walks through the Gate.
6. **The Long After / 轮后纪** — the era after his passage, when *others* inherit Aperture and the
   Codex itself is compiled. (Our "present" in-world.)
